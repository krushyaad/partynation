1. Introduction

Welcome to Party Nation! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Krush Yaad ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use our Party Nation platform ("Service").

By using Party Nation, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you use Party Nation, we collect information that you provide directly:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, profile photo (via Google or Apple Sign-In)
  • Profile Information: Username, display name, bio, profile picture, verification badge
  • Content: Posts (Drops), photos, videos, GIFs, comments, likes, and other interactions
  • Communications: Messages and interactions with other users

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain information when you use Party Nation:

  • Location Data: Your approximate location (city/region) based on IP address or GPS with your permission
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Pages viewed, features used, time spent on the platform, search queries
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, referring URLs, error logs
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to enhance your experience

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We receive information from authentication providers (Google, Apple) when you sign in, including your name, email, and profile picture.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Provide the Service: Create and manage your account, display your content, enable social features
  • Personalization: Customize your feed, show relevant content, recommend users to follow
  • Communication: Send notifications, updates, and service-related announcements
  • Safety and Security: Detect fraud, prevent abuse, enforce our Terms of Service
  • Analytics: Understand how users interact with Party Nation, improve features
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with laws, regulations, and legal requests
  • Marketing: Show relevant advertisements and sponsored content (with your consent where required)

4. How We Share Your Information

4.1 Public Information

The following information is public and visible to all Party Nation users and potentially search engines:

  • Your username, display name, profile photo, and bio
  • Posts (Drops), photos, videos, and GIFs you share
  • Comments and likes on public posts
  • Your followers and following list
  • Verification badge status

4.2 Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who help us operate Party Nation:

  • Firebase/Google Cloud: Database, authentication, hosting
  • Analytics Providers: Usage statistics and performance monitoring
  • Content Delivery Networks: Media storage and delivery

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government request, or to protect our rights, property, or safety.

4.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new owner.

4.5 With Your Consent

We may share information with third parties when you give us explicit consent to do so.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Google Cloud Platform (Firebase). Data may be processed and stored in various locations worldwide.

5.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encryption of data at rest
  • Secure authentication via Google and Apple
  • Regular security audits and monitoring
  • Access controls and permissions

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Access and Update

You can access and update your account information at any time through the Profile settings.

6.2 Delete Your Account

You can delete your account at any time through the app settings. Upon deletion, your profile and content will be removed from public view. Some information may be retained for legal or operational purposes.

6.3 Location Data

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location will prevent us from showing your city on posts.

6.4 Marketing Communications

You can opt out of promotional emails by clicking the unsubscribe link or adjusting your notification settings.

6.5 Data Portability

You can request a copy of your data by contacting us at support@krushyaad.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Party Nation is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

Users between 13 and 18 should have parental consent before using Party Nation.

10. International Data Transfers

Party Nation operates globally. Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than Jamaica. We take appropriate safeguards to ensure your data is protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
