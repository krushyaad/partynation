Party Nation

Community Guidelines

Last updated: January 2026

🇯🇲 Party Nation is Jamaica's home for sharing good vibes, celebrating culture, and connecting with the party scene. These guidelines help us maintain a positive, safe community for everyone.

✅ DO: Be Respectful

  • Treat everyone with respect and kindness
  • Celebrate diversity and different perspectives
  • Support fellow community members
  • Give credit when sharing others' content
  • Report content that violates guidelines

✅ DO: Share Great Content

  • Post original photos and videos you have rights to share
  • Tag events and locations accurately
  • Use appropriate captions and hashtags
  • Share content that adds value to the community
  • Engage authentically with comments and reactions

❌ DON'T: Post Prohibited Content

Nudity & Sexual Content

No nudity, sexually explicit content, or suggestive material involving minors. Artistic nudity may be allowed with appropriate warnings.

Violence & Gore

No graphic violence, gore, or content depicting harm to people or animals. Newsworthy content may be allowed with warnings.

Hate Speech & Discrimination

No content promoting hatred, discrimination, or violence based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin.

Harassment & Bullying

No targeted harassment, bullying, threats, doxxing, or content intended to shame or humiliate individuals.

Illegal Activities

No content promoting or facilitating illegal activities including drug sales, weapons trafficking, or fraud.

Misinformation

No deliberately false or misleading content that could cause harm, including health misinformation or election interference.

❌ DON'T: Abuse the Platform

  • Spam: Don't post repetitive, unwanted, or irrelevant content
  • Fake Engagement: Don't buy followers, likes, or use bots
  • Multiple Accounts: Don't create multiple accounts to circumvent rules
  • Impersonation: Don't pretend to be someone else or a brand you don't represent
  • Scams: Don't promote scams, pyramid schemes, or fraudulent offers
  • Coin Abuse: Don't exploit the earning system through fraudulent means

🪙 Krush Coins Guidelines

To maintain a fair economy, these activities will result in earnings suspension:

  • Creating fake accounts to earn referral bonuses
  • Using automated tools to watch ads
  • Artificially inflating reaction counts for viral bonuses
  • Trading or selling accounts with coin balances
  • Providing false information for withdrawals

🎪 Event Guidelines

  • Only post events you are authorized to promote
  • Provide accurate event information (date, time, location)
  • Update or cancel events if details change
  • Don't create fake or misleading events
  • Respect venue and organizer policies

⚖️ Enforcement

Violations may result in:

Warning

First-time minor violations may receive a warning

Content Removal

Violating content will be removed

Earnings Suspension

Coin earning may be disabled for violations

Account Suspension

Temporary or permanent account suspension

📢 Reporting

Help us keep Party Nation safe by reporting:

  • Use the report button on any post or profile
  • Provide details about the violation
  • Reports are reviewed within 24-48 hours
  • False reports may result in action against your account

For urgent safety concerns, contact: krushyaad.biz@gmail.com

🤝 Appeals

If you believe action was taken on your account in error, you can appeal by emailing krushyaad.biz@gmail.com with:

  • Your username and email
  • Description of the action taken
  • Why you believe it was in error

Appeals are typically reviewed within 7 days.

🇯🇲 One Love, One Nation

Party Nation is built on the spirit of unity, celebration, and respect. By following these guidelines, you help create a space where everyone can enjoy Jamaica's vibrant culture and connect with others who share the love for good vibes.

Thank you for being part of the nation! 🎉
